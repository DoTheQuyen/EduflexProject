Stacked diacritics — enlarged so the difference is visible

Now · Arial
ế ộ ữ ẫ ợ ằ ể ỗ ườ

Arial has no true Vietnamese design — marks are mechanically stacked, and browsers often swap to a fallback face mid-word.

Proposed · Be Vietnam Pro
ế ộ ữ ẫ ợ ằ ể ỗ ườ

Marks are drawn and spaced for the face, so accented lines keep an even rhythm and one consistent weight.

Why these two Be Vietnam Pro is drawn specifically for Vietnamese diacritics and reads cleanly in English — which is exactly the brief for a bilingual EN/VI site. Source Serif 4 keeps the serif credibility signal but has far lower stroke contrast than Playfair, so headings stay solid at 16–20px instead of thinning out. Both are open-licensed and served from the Google Fonts CDN you already use, so nothing new is self-hosted. Sizes, colours and layout are unchanged — only the two font tokens move.
