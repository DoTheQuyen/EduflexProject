Our Story

Over 5 Years of Experience

Eduflex was founded by dedicated consultants who have personally experienced the challenges of studying and living abroad. With a strong passion for education, we focus on helping overseas students achieve their dream of studying in Australia with confidence.

Led by Quyen Do and Tuyet Nguyen, our team combines more than 5 years of hands-on experience in guiding international students through university admissions, visa processes, and settlement in Australia.

We believe that education is more than just a degree — it’s a life-changing journey. That’s why our support doesn’t stop at enrollment; we continue to assist students in finding accommodation, adjusting to Australian culture, and planning for future careers.
