# Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A menu item you expected is missing | Your role does not include that permission, or it was granted after you signed in. | Sign out and back in. If it is still missing, contact your provider. |
| You are signed out unexpectedly | Your session expired. | Sign in again. |
| A form will not submit | A required question is unanswered. | Scroll up through the form and look for highlighted questions. |
| A form is read-only | It has already been submitted. | Ask staff to reopen it. |
| Your application status has not changed | Staff have not progressed it yet. | Check the notification bell, then contact your provider. |
| The **?** button opens the wrong page | The screen has no help topic assigned yet and fell back to the guide's home page. | Use the search box at the top of this site. |

Still stuck? Contact your education provider directly — Eduflex support cannot
see your provider's data.
