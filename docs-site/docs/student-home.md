## Finding the right page fast

Every screen in the portal has a **?** button in the top-right corner. Selecting
it opens the help topic for that exact screen, so you rarely need to search.

## Need something this guide does not cover?

Contact your education provider directly. They hold your records — Eduflex
support cannot see them.

## About this guide

- **Applies to:** the Eduflex Student Portal. Staff and administrator features
  are documented separately and are not covered here.
- **Illustrations** are annotated wireframes rather than screenshots. They show
  layout and controls, not live data, so they stay accurate across releases.
  Where a page has one, the numbered markers match the list beneath it.
