# Glossary

The terms you will meet in the Student Portal.

**Application**
: Your request to study a course. It moves through a series of statuses from the
  moment you submit it — see [Statuses](statuses.md).

**CoE — Confirmation of Enrolment**
: The document issued once your place in a course is confirmed. Your provider
  needs it before a student visa application can proceed.

**Enrolment**
: The record your provider creates once your application is accepted. It holds
  the course, the dates, and your progress through it.

**Form**
: A questionnaire your provider sends you, built from one of their templates.
  The questions differ between providers and course types — see
  [Submit a form request](../user/forms/submit-a-form-request.md).

**Form response**
: Your answers to one form. It has its own status, separate from the form
  itself.

**Intake**
: The start period you are applying for, for example *Feb 2027*.

**Profile**
: Your own account details — name, email and password. Your addresses and
  emergency contact are stored on each application instead, because they
  describe your situation at the time you applied.

**Provider**
: The institution or agency handling your application. They hold your records;
  Eduflex support cannot see them.
