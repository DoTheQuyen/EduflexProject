# View a student record

**Who can do this:** Admin, Manager, Staff (`StudentsView`)
**Where:** Staff Portal → **Academic** → **Students** → select a student

The student detail screen is the single place to see everything about one
person before you act on their case.

## Personal details

**Date of birth**, **Nationality**, **Passport no.** and **Address**.

## Student history

A summary of the student's journey, including their **Visa outcome** where one
has been recorded.

## Applications

**Active applications** are listed with their status. When there are none you
see *No active applications.*

## Enrolments

The student's enrolments, linking through to the full
[enrolment record](../enrolments/create-an-enrolment.md).

## Documents on file

Every document held against the student, each with a **Download** button. When
there are none you see *No documents uploaded yet.*

## Deactivate and reactivate

| Button | When it appears |
|---|---|
| **Deactivate** | The student is active and your role has `StudentsDelete`. |
| **Reactivate** | The student is inactive and your role has `StudentsEdit`. |

Deactivating preserves everything on this page. Use it rather than deleting.

::: tip Check here before creating anything
Before starting a new enrolment or application for someone, open their
record. It tells you what already exists, which is the cheapest way to
avoid duplicating work someone else has already done — and duplicate
student records are the one mistake in Eduflex that nothing later undoes.
:::
