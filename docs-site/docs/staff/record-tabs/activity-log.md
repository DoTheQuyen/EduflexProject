# Audit Trail tab

**Where:** The **Audit Trail** tab on an enrolment, a migration case or a
financial record.

A single panel headed **Activity log**: a timeline of everything that has
happened to the record, each entry showing when it happened, who did it, and
what they did.

Empty, it reads *No activity recorded yet.*

## What it is for

Three questions it answers quickly:

- **"Who changed this?"** — the entry names the person.
- **"When did this step actually complete?"** — the timestamp, not someone's
  recollection.
- **"Did we tell them?"** — cross-check against the
  [Communication tab](communication.md)'s sent history.

## What you cannot do

The log is read-only. There is no way to edit or remove an entry from the
interface, which is the point — an audit trail you can edit is not an audit
trail.

::: info It records what the system did, not what you meant
The log captures actions taken in Eduflex. A phone call, a conversation in
the office, or an email sent from your own mail client leaves no entry. If
a decision needs to be on the record, record it in Eduflex — a note on the
related [task](tasks.md), or an email sent from the
[Communication tab](communication.md).
:::
