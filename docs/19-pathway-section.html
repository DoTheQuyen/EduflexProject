Planning past your first visa

The course you pick is a migration decision

Most students choose a course, then find out later what it did to their options. These three stages are one continuous file — which is why the person choosing your course should be the person who understands stage three.

500 Student visa

While you study

You need an offer and a Confirmation of Enrolment before the visa can be lodged, and genuine-student evidence that matches the course you picked.

What we do here
Shortlist courses, secure the offer and CoE, and prepare the application.
485 Temporary Graduate

After you finish

Lets you stay and work once you graduate. It is time-bound in both directions: your qualification must meet the Australian study requirement, and the application has to be in before your student visa runs out.

What we do here
Track the deadline from day one, and keep your English test inside its validity window.
189 / 190 / 491 Skilled & sponsored

Longer term

Points-tested, state-nominated, or employer-sponsored. What you qualify for depends heavily on the occupation your qualification maps to — a decision effectively made back at stage one.

What we do here
Check your intended occupation against the course before you enrol, not after.

Choosing a cheaper course that maps to no skilled occupation can cost far more than it saves, three years later.

Before you commit

Ask what your course leads to, not just what it costs

It is a fair question to put to any agent. If the answer is vague, that tells you something — and it costs nothing to ask us the same thing.

What I deliberately left out No durations, age limits or fees appear anywhere in this diagram. Official and third-party sources currently disagree on the doctoral 485 duration (three years vs four), and the whole program changed on 1 July 2024. Numbers like that go stale silently and would be a real liability on a migration agency's own site. Subclass numbers and the shape of the pathway are stable, so that is all this states — the volatile detail stays a conversation, which also happens to be the thing you are selling.
