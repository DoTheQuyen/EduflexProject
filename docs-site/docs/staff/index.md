# Staff guide

This guide covers the day-to-day work done in the **Staff Portal**. Which menu
items you can see depends on the permissions attached to your role — if a
section described here is missing from your sidebar, ask your administrator
(see [Assign permissions](../admin/users/assign-permissions.md)).

## Start here

New to the portal? Two short pages cover most of the interface:

- [Your dashboard](dashboard.md) — what you land on after signing in.
- [Working with lists](lists.md) — search, filters, paging and why buttons come
  and go. Every management screen works the same way.
