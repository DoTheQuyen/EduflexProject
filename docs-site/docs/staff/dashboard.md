# Your dashboard

**Who can do this:** Any staff member
**Where:** Staff Portal → **Dashboard**

The landing screen after you sign in. Four panels.

## Your sidebar

| Menu item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Your overview of current work. |
| Enquiry | Incoming enquiries from prospective students. |
| Academic | Courses, Applications, Enrolments and Students. |
| Migration Cases | Visa matters and their steps. |
| Tasks | My Tasks and All Tasks. |
| Partners | Education Partners and Business Partners. |
| Finance | Accounts and Commission Records. |
| Marketing | Course Promotions and Feedback. |
| Template | Dynamic Forms, VISA Process Templates, Practitioner Tags, Email Templates, Invoice Templates. |
| Administration | Users, Roles, Departments, App Settings. |
| Profile | Your own details. |

Which of these you can see depends on your role. Select the collapse control at
the top of the sidebar to shrink it to icons. The header controls — breadcrumb,
**?**, notification bell and **Logout** — are the same as in the Student Portal
and are described in [Portal tour](../user/getting-started/portal-tour.md).

## At a Glance

Headline counts of what is currently on your plate.

## Open Items by Module

A breakdown of outstanding work per module, so you can see where the backlog
is rather than only how big it is.

When there is nothing outstanding the dashboard shows **All caught up** and
*Nothing outstanding right now.*

## Quick Access

Shortcuts into the screens you use most, saving a trip through the sidebar
groups.

## Recent Notifications

The same notifications as the bell in the header, in a readable list rather
than a dropdown. Useful first thing — the bell only shows what arrived while
you were signed in.

## What the dashboard shows depends on your role

The panels are built from the modules your role can see. If a colleague's
dashboard shows counts yours does not, the difference is permissions, not a
fault — see [Assign permissions](../admin/users/assign-permissions.md).
