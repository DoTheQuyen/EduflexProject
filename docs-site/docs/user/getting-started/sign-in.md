# Sign in

**Who can do this:** Anyone with an Eduflex account
**Before you start:** You need the email address your account was created with.

## Create an account

1. Go to the Eduflex home page.
2. Select **Register**.
3. Enter your details and complete the "I'm not a robot" check.
4. Select **Register** to submit.

**Result:** Your account is created and you are taken to the sign-in page.

## Sign in

1. Select **Login**.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Select **Login**.

**Result:** You land on your **Dashboard**. Students go to the Student Portal;
staff go to the Staff Portal.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | Cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Invalid email or password | The credentials do not match an account. | Check the email address, then re-enter the password. |
| The robot check will not complete | The page was loaded from an address the check does not recognise. | Reload the page. If it persists, contact your provider. |
| You sign in but a menu item is missing | Your role does not have that permission, or your permissions changed after you signed in. | Sign out and back in. If it is still missing, ask your administrator. |
